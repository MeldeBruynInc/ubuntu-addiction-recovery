Important Information

The Ubuntu Recovery app is intended to support personal reflection and recovery education. It is not a replacement for professional medical care, mental-health treatment, counselling, or established recovery support.

Your privacy is our priority. We do not sell your data, and your journal entries remain private. Please review our full privacy practices before using the app.
