Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 01 January 2025

Website: ubuntuaddictionrecovery.com
Contact: info@ubuntuaddictionrecovery.com

Ubuntu Recovery ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, Ubuntu Recovery, and our website, in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: When you sign up for early access or create an account, we may collect your name, email address, and subscription details.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the app, including features used, session length, and error reports.
  • Device Information: We may collect non-identifiable data such as device type, operating system, and app version.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve app functionality.
  • To manage subscriptions and premium features.
  • To communicate with you about updates, new features, and support.
  • To analyze usage trends and improve user experience.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We may share data with trusted third-party service providers (e.g., RevenueCat for subscription management) solely to support app functionality.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

4. Data Safety and Security

  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.
  • All files are stored securely and encoded in UTF-8 without BOM to ensure integrity.
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. User Rights (POPIA)

  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at info@ubuntuaddictionrecovery.com.
  • You may opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • You have the right to lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator if you believe your rights have been violated.

6. Children’s Privacy

Ubuntu Recovery is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the app and on our website with a revised effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: info@ubuntuaddictionrecovery.com
